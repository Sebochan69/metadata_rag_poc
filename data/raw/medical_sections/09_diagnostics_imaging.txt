5. Diagnostics and Imaging

5.1 Laboratory Medicine

Common tests: - Complete blood count (CBC) - Basic metabolic panel
(BMP) - Liver function tests (LFTs)

Interpretation requires clinical correlation.

5.2 Imaging Modalities

-   X-ray
-   CT scan
-   MRI
-   Ultrasound
-   PET scan

Each modality has strengths and limitations.
